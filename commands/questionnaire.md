# Questionnaire

Decision: $ARGUMENTS

No decision given? The open question the conversation or branch is stuck on. Say which.

For when the answer lives in someone's head, and that someone isn't the maintainer. Make it easy to answer from a phone between meetings.

- **Lead with what you already know** from the repo, so they don't re-explain it.
- **Ask only what they can answer** and you can't find. Group by topic. Most should be multiple choice with your recommended option marked.
- For each question, say what changes depending on the answer. A question whose answer changes nothing gets cut.
- Order by what unblocks the most.
- Markdown only. Sending it anywhere is outward-facing: **ask** first.

**Done when** every question has a reason it matters and, where possible, a recommended answer.

# Brainstorm

Topic: $ARGUMENTS

No topic given? Take the open question from the conversation or the most recent TODO, issue or spec gap in the branch. Say which.

BRAINSTORM is the mode where being wrong is cheap. Spend that.

- **Restate the problem** in one sentence and say what would count as solved. Half of brainstorms are two people solving different problems.
- **Three directions, honestly different.** Not one idea and two strawmen. Include the boring one; it wins more often than anyone admits.
- For each: what it costs, what it makes easy later, what it makes hard, and **what you'd see early if it were wrong.**
- Challenge the framing once. Is this the right problem, at the right layer, right now?
- Recommend one, with the reason, and the cheapest test that would change your mind.
- No edits to the repo unless a throwaway prototype was asked for.
- **Ask** at most one question, and only if the answer flips the recommendation.

**Done when** there is a recommendation, the reason, and the cheapest next experiment.

# Discovery

Feature: $ARGUMENTS

No feature given? The one the branch or conversation is circling. Say which.

Discovery is the cheapest phase to be wrong in. The goal is to find out what you don't know, not to sound like you know it.

- **Read before you ask.** Existing code, issues, docs and data answer half the questions people ask in kickoffs.
- **Ask what only people know:** who it's for, what problem it solves for them, how they'll judge it, what can't change. One round, grouped, each question with a recommended answer they can just accept.
- Turn answers into numbered requirements, each testable or explicitly marked as not.
- Write down the assumptions you're making; a hidden assumption is a requirement nobody agreed to.
- Open questions get an owner. No implementation until scope is confirmed.

**Done when** there's a numbered requirements note with open questions and assumptions, and the maintainer has confirmed scope or named what's missing.

# Design brief

Subject: $ARGUMENTS

No subject given? Take the feature the branch or conversation is heading toward. Say which.

A brief exists to stop three people building three different things. One page. If it needs two, the scope does.

- **The problem in the user's words,** who has it and how often. Not the solution restated as a problem.
- **What success looks like,** observable: a number, a behavior, a complaint that stops.
- **In scope and out of scope.** The out-of-scope list is the useful half.
- Constraints that actually bind: platform, access, performance, data, deadline. Skip the ones that don't.
- Open questions, each with an owner and a recommended answer.
- No UI drawing unless asked; a brief that picks the layout has skipped a step.
- **Ask** only about the problem and success; the rest you can draft and let them correct.

**Done when** the brief fits on one page and has a success measure and an out-of-scope list.
